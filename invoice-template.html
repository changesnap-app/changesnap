Why it matters

Send the invoice the day the job is done, while the customer is happy. Put the due date and how to pay on it. If the final price differs from the quote, attach the signed change order so nobody argues about the difference.

Questions

What should an invoice include?

Your business name and contact, the customer, an invoice number, the date, line items, tax if it applies, the total, the due date and how to pay.

How soon should I send it?

The same day the work is finished. Late invoices get paid late.

Does this store my data?

No. The page runs in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.

Can I accept payments here?

No. This is a document template. Add your own payment instructions in the notes.
