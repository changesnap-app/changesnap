Product: ChangeSnap
Category: B2B SaaS / contractor workflow software
Live site: https://changesnap.onrender.com/

Short description: Create a priced change order, send a secure customer approval link, collect a signature, and preserve an immutable approval record before extra work begins.

Long description: ChangeSnap is mobile-first change-order software for solo residential contractors. Contractors enter the customer, scope, labor, materials, tax, schedule impact and payment terms. ChangeSnap calculates the total and creates a unique approval page the customer can sign. Once approved, the record is time-stamped and locked against duplicate approval. Contractors can print or save the record for their files.

Products:
1. One signed approval - $9 one-time. One customer approval link and signed record, no subscription.
2. ChangeSnap Unlimited - $19/month recurring. Unlimited approval links, approval history and branded documents. Cancel before next renewal.

Free allowance: first 3 approval links, no card.
