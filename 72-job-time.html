Job totals

Completed, non-voided sessions only. Running time and unpaid breaks are not included. Totals are recorded work time, not payroll or a bill.

Corrections keep their prior values and reason. Void removes time from totals without deleting its history. Times are stored in UTC and displayed in your browser's timezone. Each session is limited to 36 hours; overlapping sessions are refused. CSV includes all records, including voided records. This is not a payroll, overtime, billing or attendance-verification system.
