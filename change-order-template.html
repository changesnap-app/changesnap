Why it matters

A change order is a short written record that the job changed and the customer agreed. It protects you from unpaid extra work and protects the customer from surprise bills. Write one whenever the customer asks for something outside the original quote, you find a hidden problem, or materials force a substitution.

Get the signature before you start the extra work, not after.

Questions

What should a change order include?

What changed, the cost change, the schedule change, a reference to the original job, the date, and a signature or other dated approval from the customer.

Does the customer have to sign it?

A signature or clear written yes (text or email) with a date is what protects you. Ask for it before the extra work starts.

Can I use this for any trade?
