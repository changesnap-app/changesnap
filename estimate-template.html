Why it matters

A clear written estimate stops arguments about what was included. List the scope in plain words, break the price into lines, and say how long the price is good for. If the job changes later, write a change order instead of just doing the work.

Questions

What should an estimate include?

Your business and the customer, the job address, the date, a plain description of the work, line items with prices, tax if it applies, and how long the price is valid.

What is the difference between an estimate and a quote?

An estimate is your best price for the described work. A quote is usually a firm price. Say which one yours is in the notes.
